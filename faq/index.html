FAQ / Contact

Good to know.

For ordering questions, text (929) 742-4202 or email islanddelicacy@outlook.com.

Can I get a plate today?

No — and that's on purpose. Everything is cooked fresh to order, so orders close at 10 AM for next-day pickup.

What pickup time do I choose?

Choose a date only. We'll text you the day of to set your pickup time.

How do I pay?

Payment runs through Square once the official item links are added. Until then, the order button prepares a text order.

Do you deliver?

Delivery is planned for larger orders with a $15 fee, pending owner confirmation. Text the business line for availability.

What's in a plate?

Most plates include rice & peas plus exactly two sides; rasta pasta plates offer rice & peas as a side choice instead. Extra meat is $10, extra oxtail is $12, and standalone sides are $5 each.

How do I contact Island Delicacy?

Text or call (929) 742-4202, or email islanddelicacy@outlook.com. We never route orders to a personal number.
