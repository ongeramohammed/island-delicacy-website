# Island Delicacy Brand + Image Guide

This is an owner-facing guide for future updates. It is not part of the customer-facing homepage.

## Photography priorities

1. **Homepage hero** — 3:2 source, 2400×1600 preferred. Signature plate plus founder/cook in a warm real prep environment.
2. **Social share image** — 1200×630. One strong dish, logo, one-line promise.
3. **Signature dish cards** — 4:3, 1600×1200 preferred. Oxtail, jerk chicken, curry goat, oxtail rasta pasta, escovitch fish, curry shrimp.
4. **Mobile/social story images** — 4:5, 1080×1350 preferred.
5. **Process/authenticity shots** — 3:2. Seasoning, plating, steam, sauce brushing, trays, hands at work.
6. **Service/catering banner** — 16:9, 1600×900 preferred. Trays, family platters, event pans, abundance.

## Visual style

- Deep green, warm gold, cream, and rich food tones.
- Real food texture: steam, char, sauce, herbs, peppers, fluffy rice, fresh cabbage/slaw.
- Premium but local: confident, warm, trustworthy, and uncluttered.
- Use flyers and business-card graphics only as supporting proof, never as the main food experience.

## AI image rules if temporary images are unavoidable

- No visible fake text inside images.
- No fake apron logos or fake menus in the background.
- No fake hands unless anatomy is checked closely.
- Avoid generic restaurant clichés and plastic-looking food.
- Review every output at 200% zoom before publishing.

## Logo usage cleanup

- Use the current logo sparingly: header, contact card, share card.
- Prefer the wordmark/name in text for premium sections.
- Future brand cleanup should create: primary wordmark, small emblem, one-color version, dark-background version, and social avatar.
