How preorder works

Order today. Pick it up tomorrow.

  1. 01

    Build your plate

    Pick a plate, exactly two sides, quantity and a pickup date. Most plates include rice & peas; rasta pasta plates take it as a side instead.

  2. 02

    Check it, then pay

    You see every plate, side, extra and leave-off request written out before anything is sent. Then you pay securely through Square — no account needed.

  3. 03

    We text your time

    You choose the date; we text you on pickup day to set the time. The kitchen takes up to 30 plates per pickup day.
